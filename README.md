## 📌 Featured Projects

### 1. Sales Performance Analysis
**Problem:** Declining sales in certain regions with no clear reason.
**Tools Used:** Python, Pandas, Power BI, Excel
**Key Insight:** Identified top 3 underperforming regions and product categories. Recommended targeted promotions that could improve sales by **18%**.

### 2. Customer Churn Analysis
**Problem:** High customer drop-off rate for a subscription service.
**Tools Used:** SQL, Python, Tableau
**Key Insight:** Found that customers with low engagement in first 30 days were 3x more likely to churn. Built a churn-risk dashboard for early intervention.

### 3. E-Commerce Dashboard
**Problem:** Management needed a real-time view of business KPIs.
**Tools Used:** Power BI, SQL, DAX
**Key Insight:** Built an interactive dashboard tracking Revenue, Orders, AOV & Customer Retention — reducing manual reporting time by **70%**.
